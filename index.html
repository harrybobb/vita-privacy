Privacy policy

Vita: Anatomy & Physiology (Android app ID com.harshavardhanpedada.vita)
Effective 20 October 2026

Summary

Vita does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or usage data. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your phone.

What is stored on your phone

To work, Vita saves your study progress on your device only:

  • answered questions, scores, mastered items and your quiz streak
  • notes you have marked as read, and your settings (level, light or dark mode)

This data never leaves the phone through the app. You can delete it at any time in the app (Progress, then Reset all progress), by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling Vita.

Android backup

If you have turned on Android's own backup for your Google account, Android may include Vita's saved progress in that backup so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup belongs to your Google account and is managed by Android; the developer cannot see or access it. You can turn this off in your phone's backup settings.

No third parties

Vita contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or other third-party software development kits. It does not request any Android permissions and does not access your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

Children

Vita is designed for college and university students and is not directed at children under 13.

Medical disclaimer

Vita is a study aid for education only. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page and inside the app with a new effective date.
